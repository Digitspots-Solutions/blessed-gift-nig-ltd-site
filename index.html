About Us

Sisi Alero Fintech Innovations (Sisi Alero Pay App Nigeria Limited) is a leading financial technology company based in Owerri Municipal, Nigeria. Founded and led by experienced Digital Payments Strategists, we are committed to driving financial inclusion across Nigeria by providing accessible, secure, and innovative digital payment solutions.

Our platform enables individuals, small businesses, and enterprises to conduct financial transactions with confidence, speed, and efficiency. We understand the unique challenges of the Nigerian financial landscape and have built our solutions to address them directly.

From peer-to-peer transfers to merchant payments, bill settlements to airtime purchases, Sisi Alero Pay simplifies every financial interaction. Our mission is to empower every Nigerian with the tools they need to participate fully in the digital economy, regardless of location or economic status.
